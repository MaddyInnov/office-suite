Why it's different

Not another rent-per-seat clone. Four structural advantages the big suites can't copy without changing what they are.

✦ One prompt, whole workflow

"Prepare my monthly business review pack" builds the sales workbook, the review deck, the summary report, a CRM lead, the follow-up tasks and the announcement draft — finished work, not autocomplete.

🏠 Your instance, your data

Office Suite runs on hardware you control. Documents are never on our servers because there is no "our servers" in the loop. It works offline and air-gapped.

⚙️ Works with no AI at all

Deterministic engines generate and recalculate real Office files with zero marginal cost. Plug in your own AI key if you want polish — never required, never pooled.

📗 Editing loses nothing

Open a workbook that has charts, images, conditional formatting, validation and comments — edit it — save it — everything is still there. Measured on a fidelity corpus: 27/27, zero known loss.

📝 Real collaboration

Docs track changes with accept/reject review, live sync with presence, and Sheets saves that merge instead of overwriting a colleague's work.

🇮🇳 Built for Bharat's businesses

Coaching institutes, clinics, traders and schools: ready-made packs for admissions, fee registers, stock and results — priced for India, not converted from dollars.
